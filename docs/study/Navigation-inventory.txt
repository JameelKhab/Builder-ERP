FACTUAL NAVIGATION FINDINGS AND LIMITS

1. The admin interface mixes broad domains (Finance, Projects), individual work
   queues (Project requisitions, Site receipts, Site diaries), commercial steps
   (Payment certificates, Advances & retention releases, Final accounts & closeout)
   and specialist feeds (Labour & plant actual feeds, P6 schedule progress) at the
   same top level. Evidence: full42-entry inventory in snapshot41.
2. Four-level menu paths are observed; their deepest leaves include dashboard
   settings and CRM configuration. This does not mean every operational task
   requires four levels or four clicks.
3. Repeated labels are verified; duplicate underlying destinations are not. The
   AX menu buttons expose labels and hierarchy, not target URLs/action identities.
   Products under Reporting may be an analysis view; Customers under Finance may
   differ in scope from a CRM customer view. Verify identity, filters, permissions
   and actions before consolidating any two routes.
4. Project-related destinations are present but partly concealed under More. In
   the initial My Company11-villa project, More reveals WBS/Budgets, Contracts,
   Requisitions, Site materials, Service entries, Client certificates, Variations,
   Final accounts, Award estimate and Set-up checklist (07). In the Site Engineer
   Marina Gate project, More reveals a role-limited set: WBS/Budgets, Contracts,
   Requisitions and Client certificates (33). Do not imply all users see all links.
5. Existing record links offer useful continuity. The estimate has Opportunity,
   Quotation and Project links plus version/history and a handover message (05);
   the project breadcrumb returns to the originating estimate (06). A new design
   should retain this lineage; repeated contextual links are not duplicate records.

REVISED NAVIGATION MAP - REVISION 3 PROPOSAL
This replaces the earlier candidate grouping. Full explanation: PDF pages 2-13.
Global: My Work | Tenders | Projects | Company.
Within a project: Overview | Plan | Site | Buying | Costs & Contracts | Documents | Handover.
Company directory: Buying | Finance | People | Assets & Fleet | Stores | Contacts & Catalogue | Reports.
Administration is a separate utility. Contextual views open canonical records, not copies.
The map is a proposal; no staff usability test or new live audit occurred in Revision 3.

Current primary entry | Proposed home | Continuity rule

01. Dashboard
  Home: My Work; Projects > Overview
  Rule: Personal actions and project health have separate purposes; retire the generic duplicate entry.

02. Reports
  Home: Company > Reports; relevant workspace
  Rule: One report definition; contextual views carry filters. Report setup belongs in Administration.

03. My Approvals & Activities
  Home: My Work
  Rule: One decision/task queue, with explicit action, project, owner and due date.

04. CRM
  Home: Tenders > Opportunities
  Rule: Keep leads, client follow-ups and bid decisions together. Contacts link to the shared directory.

05. Tender & BOQ
  Home: Tenders > Tender workspace
  Rule: Tender documents, take-off, estimate revisions and bid review remain together.

06. Sales & Quotations
  Home: Tenders > Offers & award
  Rule: Preserve quotation versions and accepted terms. Non-project sales, if needed, remain a named Company workflow.

07. Projects
  Home: Projects
  Rule: Portfolio -> project overview -> stable project areas. Tasks appear in My Work and their project.

08. Project requisitions
  Home: Project > Buying > Requests
  Rule: Site action Request materials opens the same request with location and work item filled in.

09. Project set-up
  Home: Project > Overview > Setup checklist
