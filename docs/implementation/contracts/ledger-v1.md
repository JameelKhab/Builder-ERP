## Records and invariants
- A legal company owns its account chart, non-overlapping periods, numbered journal register, entries, original line snapshots and immutable events. A manual journal has one optional project, one transaction currency and a dated/source-labelled conversion into functional currency. No cross-company or mixed-project journal.
- Account code/type are fixed. Names/status may change prospectively, with version/reason/history; posting preserves code/name/type snapshots; changing an account version after preparation requires return and revision. General accounts allow manual journals. Bank, AP, AR, tax, stock and other control roles are reserved for typed subledgers and reject manual postings. No company gets an invented production chart or opening balances.
- Draft -> submitted -> posted, or submitted -> returned -> revised draft; author may withdraw or cancel before posting. Only the author edits. Submission freezes the reviewed version. An active independent ledger approver with explicit separate company-currency journal limit posts atomically after rechecking access, accounts, open period, source link and balance. Existing purchase/budget limits grant no ledger limit. Retry/stale versions never duplicate entries/numbers.
- At least two lines, one positive debit OR credit per line, exact 2-decimal amounts, equal transaction totals. AED/USD/EUR are the supported 2-decimal currencies. Foreign entries require explicit accounting rate/date/source; procurement quotes are not accounting rates. Convert each side at the same rate, round half-up, allocate residual cents to its largest line (first in tie), record that adjustment, and verify positive/base balance. Never silently conceal rounding or infer rates.
- Post allocates a unique company/year sequence inside the same transaction as frozen ledger lines and event; no reset/delete/edit of posted headers or lines. Full reversal creates a separate linked, independently approved document at original amounts/rate with opposite sides; original posting remains. One active reversal per original, only same/later accounting date, open period and current posting authority. No reversing a reversal; corrections after reversal are new journals with explicit evidence.
- Period close/reopen requires company-wide finance configuration authority, a reason and optimistic version. Close blocks pending submitted journals in that period. Reopen is an audited independent decision by another controller; it does not rewrite dates/facts. Posted facts cannot move to another period.
- Manual journal evidence is a required reviewable source reference plus explanation. It is not a file-retention or proof-authenticity system. Tax, AP/AR, bank, stock and automated source posting are future typed integrations, explicitly blocked from general manual control accounts.

## Reports and controls
Company/project ledger and trial balance use all authorised posted lines, including reversals by their actual date, with accounting-date opening/movement/closing reconciliation. No draft enters accounting totals. Journal detail/search/work queues/exports all enforce current scope; project-only viewers cannot see company-unallocated or other-project lines. Limited scopes are labelled, never called the full legal-company trial balance. Findings precede report rows and export, with population, cutoff, debit-credit equation, pending decisions, reversal counts and missing opening/localisation caveats. Ledger is distinct from earlier illustrative cost/cash values and operational material cost facts; no duplicate additions to construction cost reports.

## Required validation
Exact foreign-rounding/balance; author independence and separate limits; period/account revocation at posting; concurrent versions/duplicate reversals; immutable historical account/rate/detail; cutoff reversal and opening reconciliation; company/project/search/export/preview scope; local authenticated HTTP posting; additive examples; preserve previous database rows/columns and recovery blobs; desktop/mobile and source-ledger navigation. These prove this subset, not full accounting or migration parity.
