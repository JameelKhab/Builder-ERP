Source70 and fresh read-only bill213 show supplier, reference, bill/accounting/due dates, currency, PO matching, service/certificate links, quantity/unit/price/account/tax, deductions, posted journal items and remaining due. This increment connects existing typed **material receipts** to supplier bills and supplier return credits. Service/subcontract measurement, certificate retention/advance recovery, non-PO charges and legal invoice localisation remain separate typed dependencies. A bill is not a cash payment or a new physical stock movement. Accounting classification is explicitly chosen from the company's expense/asset general accounts; stock valuation/GRNI recognition is not silently invented. The bill report identifies this boundary, unbilled receipts and billed returns awaiting credit.

## Data and rules

- Company/project/order/supplier/transaction currency derive from the issued order and immutable master snapshots. One bill uses one order/project/currency. Source reference is required and normalised per company/supplier/document kind to prevent duplicates; author/dates/source explanations and supporting accounting references are retained. No bank instruction is accepted by this workflow.
- Bill lines identify accepted receipt lots, originating order line/product/UOM/WBS/location, invoice quantity, explicit unit price/price basis, accounting classification and an effective company tax code. Duplicate receipt rows in a bill are rejected. Quantities respect product precision and total net accepted less already-posted net billed quantities, rechecked under a transaction at posting. Drafts reserve nothing. Rejected stock never becomes billable.
- Enter invoice price per explicitly chosen base quantity, with exact ratio calculation for pack prices; do not derive a rounded base rate then multiply. Record quote-versus-invoice net variance and a required explanation whenever different. Accounting FX has its own date/source/rate, independent of order FX. Dates are explicit; accounting date cannot precede bill date or be future; due date cannot precede bill date. Use original order payment-day snapshot as a suggestion only; unknown terms remain unknown.
- Tax codes are explicit company configuration: exclusive percentage, recoverable percentage, effective range, purpose and optional tax asset account. Zero tax also needs a named reason/category. No jurisdiction/rate default is inferred. Freeze the code/rate/recovery and account versions in the bill; changes invalidate preparation. Only this arithmetic is implemented; tax returns, inclusive/compound/withholding and fiscal positions remain open.
- Draft -> submitted -> posted, or returned -> revised draft; author can withdraw/cancel before posting. Independent AP posting authority is separately assigned (default zero); it is not purchase/budget/general-journal authority. Recheck current scope, all account versions, tax effectiveness, period, supplier identity/order snapshot and quantity availability. Returned/blocked-for-new-buying suppliers can still have liabilities recorded against existing orders; no new purchasing privilege is implied.
- Posting atomically creates immutable bill/line facts and one linked balanced journal: debit chosen line classification plus nonrecoverable tax; debit recoverable tax; credit AP control. Preserve per-line transaction and functional amounts/rounding, account snapshots and unique source link. The original general-journal API cannot edit/post/reverse typed AP sources. Their corrections must maintain the AP subledger.
- Supplier return is operational and does not automatically create credit/cash. A return-credit document links one posted bill, its original receipt lines and supplier credit reference. It can credit only currently overbilled returned quantity and remaining original quantity, rechecked at posting. Use original tax/classification/rate and allocate remaining original transaction/base values proportionately, with residual cents on the final credit, so totals never exceed or strand the original. Credits do not create stock returns. Price-only credits, credit-without-return, full-document voids and debit notes remain open, clearly labelled.
- Posted documents and evidence stay immutable; credits are additional facts. No editing or deleting posted bills, credits, journal rows or history. Stale/retry/duplicate operations do not duplicate payable balances or journal numbers.
