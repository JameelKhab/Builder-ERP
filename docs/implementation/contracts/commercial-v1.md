## Connected original workflow

Approved current estimate → versioned offer draft → independent commercial approval → record dated client acceptance evidence and project/BOQ allocation → independent award verification → immutable original client contract awaiting finance configuration. No external sending, signing, invoicing or cash action is performed. Project/WBS/baseline setup remains independently accessible from the estimate; award uses an existing same-company project and explicit item mapping. Released budget never changes.

An offer family belongs to one estimate study; only one working/current offer, one accepted award. A revision supersedes an approved offer and uses the latest approved estimate from the same study. Accepted offers require the later controlled contract-change workflow; they cannot be revised or cancelled here. Draft edits and decisions retain snapshots. Approved fields/lines cannot change; only controlled terminal states change. Submitted drafts can be withdrawn, returned or approved. Approved offers can be withdrawn with a reason if no pending acceptance. Cancelled history is retained.

Each offer retains legal-company/customer identity, source estimate revision, currency/dated rate, exact BOQ quantity/unit and line selling amount, cost and pricing lineage. Selling amounts are authoritative rounded source amounts; displayed six-decimal unit rates are explanatory, never multiplied again to silently alter value. Summary groups are not billed. No inferred unit conversion or selling reprice. Different negotiated prices need an approved estimate revision. Values are exclusive of tax; VAT, tax accounts and certificate finance configuration are a subsequent controlled stage.

Terms: offered date, inclusive validity date, description, payment days from invoice date, retention percentage/cap, advance ceiling, recovery percentage, inclusions and exclusions. Retention and advance are ceilings, not receivable/cash balances. Percentage 0–100, money nonnegative <= net offer, explicit zero; recovery is positive when advance >0. Payment days 0–365. Approval cannot occur after validity, nor before offered date. Acceptance date is between approval/offered date and validity, <= today. Late recording of in-time acceptance is visibly flagged and independently verified; acceptance after expiry is blocked and requires a revised offer.

## Access and state rules

Separate sales.read/write/approve capabilities. Commercial approval limit defaults to zero for every existing member and is administered through reviewed access. It covers the company-currency net offer value, independently of estimate/purchase/budget/ledger authority. Self-review blocked. Award verifier differs from offer author and evidence recorder; can be the original independent commercial approver. Current author, source estimate approver, offer approver and verifier authority, active customer and unchanged customer identity are checked at relevant gates. Source estimate must remain the latest approved study revision; pending estimate revision blocks stale offers. Scope enforced on detail/history/evidence/download/search/report/export. Project assignment cannot extend an actor's authority. Preview is read-only.

Acceptance includes reference, date, named client signatory, a local retained evidence file and explicit line→WBS/location allocation in one project. Reference unique per company/customer to prevent accidental repeated awards; returned/cancelled attempts retain it but do not reserve it. Every leaf maps once to an active item WBS with matching cost code; location optional and scoped. Current mapping snapshots are rechecked at award. Files <=2 MiB, retained raw with SHA256, attachment-only download. No URL fetch or file execution. Submitted evidence is immutable; return/cancel permits a new attempt. Optimistic revisions apply; atomic award and contract creation are idempotent. A retry cannot create duplicate contracts or overwrite acceptance.

## Reporting and acceptance evidence

Company/project report: latest offer per study, scope/period/state/as-of declared, separate open approved potential and awarded contract totals. Exclude superseded/withdrawn from active totals; use saved company rates, never add currencies. Findings precede detail and CSV; original contract value is not revenue, incurred cost or cash. Full authorised filtered rows feed findings. No external AI provider; deterministic explanations retain source IDs.
