## Original model and transitions

- An original awarded contract remains immutable. Versioned finance policies select company AR, retention and revenue accounts, explicit exclusive tax rate/basis/account and a reason. A second finance controller with sufficient separate certificate authority approves activation. The policy freezes account identity/version and original commercial terms; replacing it never rewrites prior measurements. Existing users receive zero new financial authority.
- One unresolved certificate per contract avoids overlapping applications. Draft → technical → commercial → finance → approved. A stage reviewer differs from the author and every earlier reviewer in that cycle. Technical review uses project-manager capability; commercial and finance approval additionally need separate certificate limits in company currency. A return preserves history and requires author revision/new submission cycle. Unapproved work may be cancelled; approved work cannot be reset or silently reduced.
- Each certificate includes all original BOQ leaves exactly once, an installed cumulative quantity, measurement period, dated FX evidence, explanatory basis and an immutable original local evidence file. Cumulative quantity is between previous approved and contracted quantity. Current line value = rounded exact cumulative fraction of authoritative original line amount minus previously approved value. This preserves final residual cents and avoids treating a rounded displayed unit price as authoritative.
- The predecessor is the latest approved certificate, not the latest draft or posted invoice. Periods cannot overlap approved periods. Submission snapshots predecessor, policy, totals and evidence. Each review revalidates quantities, accounts, policy, preceding reviewers' current authority and author scope. The last stage freezes quantities and deductions atomically.
- Retention is cumulative gross × original retention percentage, capped by the original contract retention ceiling, minus prior retained amount. Advance allowance is not cash. Until the connected client-advance subledger is implemented, actual eligible advance balance is explicitly zero and no recovery can be entered or inferred. This dependency remains open under F092, not represented by a fabricated opening balance. Variations, BOQ revisions, other deductions, negative corrections and release/final-account workflows remain separately tracked.
- Finance approval creates no accounting or cash. An AR preparer explicitly creates a draft invoice from one approved certificate. Invoice dates, due date and accounting FX are separately recorded. Submit → independent AR posting with a separate zero-default AR limit. Posting derives a balanced journal privately from the frozen certified gross, retention and tax; neither HTTP input nor general journals can override the source. Exactly one invoice per certificate; repeat creation/posting is idempotent for the authorised actor. Posted invoices are immutable. Linked credit/correction and customer receipts follow as separate connected increments; until available, no reset or manual settlement is allowed.
- Tax is an explicit configured exclusive rate on gross or net after retention/recovery; the choice and reason are visible. This is a local calculation policy, not jurisdictional tax advice or localisation certification. Two decimal minor units remain the supported currency assumption. Invoice tax derives from the certificate's frozen policy; dated invoice FX controls accounting carrying values.

## Scope, information and validation

Scoped certificate access is separate from sales cost/estimate access. AR clerks see customer accounting without supplier, general-ledger, people or access administration. Project and company views refer to the same records. Every report uses the full authorised filtered population, reports pending/certified/invoiced/retained amounts separately, groups transaction currencies, retains company carrying values and reconciles posted invoice AR/retention to the typed ledger. No amount is labelled received or cleared without its own future cash evidence.

Use one Client certificates destination; contract → finance readiness → measurement → named next reviewer → invoice. Show current gross, retention, tax and due amount before expandable BOQ, evidence, policy and history. Keep filters usable on errors; export the same complete findings and rows. Examples must cover all supplied projects/companies and distinguish synthetic facts. Verify stage independence, zero authority, live access/version changes, overmeasurement, prior/cumulative rounding, tax/FX, duplicate/retry, periods, evidence downloads, report scope/control reconciliation, immutability, browser workflow and database recovery before accepting this increment. Full 267-function parity/migration remains open.
