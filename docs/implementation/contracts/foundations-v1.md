# Company, project, work and budget contract v1

Original Builder implementation, linked to D06/D08/D19/D21, M01–M04/M10/M24/M32 and F070/F073/F074/F249/F250/F267. Source evidence: retained UI captures 06,25,26,31–33,88,90; fresh 29 October budget revision form (`docs/validation/foundations/source-budget-revision.txt`). No source application code was read. Navigation/visible help are evidence of intended behaviour, not tested enforcement. Source revision form exposes WBS, derived company/project, date, proposed budget, approved delta, reason, approver/time and Draft→Submitted→Approved/Rejected; help states immutable approved fact and protection against commitments/actuals. Source forecast approvals are distinct records and remain a separate next increment.

- Company identity has one fixed functional currency. New companies start without business grants. Organisation administration is separate from business and access administration. No automatic access expansion on company creation. Two-decimal currencies only until minor-unit support is expanded; no inferred tax rules.
- Projects belong permanently to one company, have a unique company code, dates, accountable manager, customer reference, provenance and descriptive site location. New direct-award projects start in setup with no approved budget; estimate handover is not simulated. Completion/closeout is not granted by a simple metadata edit.
- Locations have stable IDs and scoped, cycle-free parent hierarchies. Work packages have stable scoped IDs, cost codes and either group or item kind. Groups roll up children and are never procured or counted twice. Metadata edits are version checked and audited; no deletion.
- Original budget is fixed after the initial approved baseline. Existing synthetic opening balances remain explicitly opening data, not fabricated historical approvals. New projects require a complete item baseline before any revision. New items after baseline have original zero and require revisions before release.
- A budget document contains multiple item targets: proposed revised budget and proposed released capacity. At submission, freeze the current project budget revision and a snapshot of each original/current/released balance. Approval checks this snapshot again; parallel revisions cannot overwrite one another.
- Submitted documents are immutable. Return permits a new draft version; author can cancel before approval. Approval requires a different actor, a live project grant, budget.approve capability and a dedicated monetary limit. Authority is measured on the sum of absolute budget/release changes (the larger change per line), never the net offset. The reason, approval, changes and source IDs remain append-only.
- Approved facts add original/budget/release deltas exactly once. Target revised budget cannot be below total committed cost or incurred cost; released capacity cannot be below held local reservations plus issued local commitments, or exceed revised budget. Opening released capacity is explicit and distinct from opening commitments. Approval dates come from the server UTC clock; there is no user-entered backdating or future-effective funding in this increment. Company timezone, period and historic-cutoff policies remain open.
- Exact decimal amounts, company functional currency, maximum two decimals. No automatic FX revaluation of a company budget. Consumption, cash and forecasts do not change when budgets are approved.
- Budgets and audit detail require cost permission. Quantity-only users see location/work structure without cost facts. Company rollups include only permitted project rows; all findings use that full scoped population.
- Real migration, accounting-period controls, approved forecast versions, detailed resource rate build-ups, change-request links and original-source equivalence remain open. Tests must exercise real state transitions, concurrent revisions, cross-company/project scope, hierarchy cycles, independent approval, allowance protection, atomicity and source reconciliation.
