## Distinct correction intents

1. **Annul an approved advance:** independent finance review withdraws the request's effective approval and, if invoiced, credits the entire original invoice. Any certified recovery must first be corrected. The original approval, invoice, receipts and allocations remain immutable. A completed annulment releases its original ceiling use; recovery alone never does.
2. **Annul a certified period:** obtain technical, commercial and finance review, then withdraw its effective measurement and release its recovery allocations. If the certificate was invoiced, the same atomic operation posts its full credit. A new measurement is a new record with fresh reviews, not a reset of the old record. Later certified periods depend on the original cumulative quantities: unwind dependent periods in reverse order, or use the separately required forward adjustment workflow. Never silently invalidate descendants or double-count replacement quantities.
3. **Partial/forward adjustment:** revised measured quantities, commercial price/tax adjustments and credit allocations need explicit item-level reason and signed quantities/values, separate from a full annulment. This remains required; full annulment is not a substitute for it.
4. **Settle a customer credit:** a credit does not erase money received. Paid amounts remain as a separately labelled customer credit until a reviewed allocation to another eligible invoice or an evidenced refund settles it. Refund approval, actual cash recording and bank matching remain distinct. An erroneous receipt reversal is not a customer-refund shortcut. This connection remains required before full customer-credit acceptance.

## First connected implementation: full annulment

An immutable correction record references exactly one approved advance or certificate and its exact invoice, if any. Draft → technical → commercial → finance → applied for certificates; draft → finance → applied for advances. Return/revise and cancel preserve evidence/history. Only one live/applied correction can refer to a source; a cancelled request permits a new request. Application is atomic with the credit journal and effective source withdrawal. Repeated application cannot post twice.

The correction author needs current source-write authority (certificate preparation or AR preparation for measured work; advance preparation for advances). Technical/commercial/finance reviewers are different people from the author and each other. Recheck every earlier reviewer's live company/project scope and authority. Commercial and certificate-finance review use the existing separate certificate limit; advance review uses the existing advance limit. Posting a credit additionally needs the separate AR limit for the full original journal exposure. Limits remain zero by default; purchasing, manual journal, receipt and administrative authority never grant credit authority. Original authors/reviewers may have left: their inactive account does not prohibit a new independently reviewed correction of immutable historic facts. Lost correction-author handover remains separately required.

Each transition rechecks the source, current invoice version/state, settlement allocations, approved receipt plans, recovery dependencies, current effective predecessor and original source amounts. Source changes invalidate the prepared basis; return and revise. A current reviewer can return a stale request without accepting its facts. Drafts do not reserve money. No correction may apply while an approved receipt/reversal is waiting to record against that invoice. Pending measurement on the same contract must first be returned/cancelled or completed; subsequent certified periods must be addressed first. Explain the blocking record IDs rather than a generic error.

The effective date cannot precede the source, invoice, recorded settlements or prior correction dependencies, and cannot be future. Credit posting needs an open company period. A full credit reverses each original transaction and functional-currency line exactly, including historical advance principal, original-tax release, retention, revenue, tax FX and rounding. It does not recalculate at today's FX rate or reverse a bank journal. Inactive original accounts may receive their exact typed reversal; their company/type/control identity must remain valid. Manual general journals cannot post or reverse these typed controls.

No original row is deleted, reset or rewritten. Approved measurement lines and recovery allocations remain frozen. Effective queries exclude allocations withdrawn by an applied correction; accounting reports net the credit only at its effective accounting date. An unposted source invoice is retained but cannot subsequently be edited/submitted/posted once its source is annulled. A corrected advance cannot become eligible for recovery through a backdated new certificate.

## Balances, reports and user flow

Keep original invoice, credited amount, net recorded cash, debit outstanding and customer credit separate. Signed net AR is original invoice minus credits minus net receipts; negative AR must be labelled customer credit, not negative overdue debt or paid revenue. Before the credit date, reports retain the original charge/recovery. At and after it, principal/tax/AR/retention/revenue reconcile to original plus credit journals while cash and bank matches remain unchanged. Current operational status is explicitly separate from accounting cutoff.

The canonical source detail leads to one correction form showing impact, blockers, owner and next decision. Source links go to the original invoice/certificate/advance, credit journal and retained evidence/history. The correction register, My Work, search, project/company findings and CSV must enforce the same scope. Illustrate unbilled cancellation, unpaid credit, partially paid credit, recovered-certificate credit, dependency blocking and returned/revised review across supplied companies/projects. Do not expose an incomplete user journey as a finished feature.

Acceptance requires service and individual HTTP tests; exact same/foreign currency and final-cent reversals; paid/unpaid/partially paid credit; certified-but-uninvoiced recovery release; last/earlier certificate dependencies and replacement measurement; stale/revoked/overlapping reviewers; cross-company/project/privacy; closed periods and atomic failures; unchanged receipts/bank matches; cutoff reconciliation; effective UI/report/export/examples; and a non-destructive saved upgrade/restore. Partial forward adjustments, refund/reallocation, retention/final-account dependencies and representative-user acceptance stay open until individually implemented and evidenced.
