# Supplier payments, refunds and bank evidence — v1 contract

29 October 2026. Original local implementation design for F129, F133–F136, M01/M23/M24/M32. Related F004/F124/F139 remain dependencies. Source: retained study observations 75/91/92 and fresh read-only JAKH journal 456 / payment 112 in `validation/payments/source`. Source shows invoice, journal, direction, party, amount/currency, date, memo, bank journal and manual method. A visible Paid status does not prove bank-statement reconciliation or approval enforcement. No source mutation or Odoo code.

## Record and authority

One payment is one company, supplier, invoice currency, bank account and date; allocations identify posted bills and projects. Multiple bills and projects are supported; a batch across projects needs company-wide authority. Project users see only their payments and allocated report lines, never other projects' amounts. Functional currency, source bill/control snapshots and bank amounts are explicit. Bank register records a local accounting alias/currency/control account, not executable routing credentials.

Draft → submitted → independently approved → recorded. Return/withdraw/cancel retain history. Approval creates no cash or journal. Recording is an offline statement of a transaction performed outside Builder ERP, requiring reference and evidence explanation; no bank integration or transfer is executed. Recorder must differ from approver. Approval limit is separate, zero by default, and covers the larger of carrying amount and bank functional value. Live scopes/limits, versions, active accounts, dates, open period and available balances are rechecked atomically. Approved allocations reserve availability, drafts do not. Source changes invalidate approval and require withdrawal/revision. Exact duplicate bank reference per bank is rejected.

## Settlement and corrections

Each allocation consumes at most the current positive payable; supplier refunds consume negative balances created by posted credits after payment. Negative balances remain visible. Amounts use decimal arithmetic, two-decimal AED/USD/EUR only. Partial allocation uses the current remaining transaction and carrying balances, final allocation exhausts residual cents. Allocations cannot precede supporting invoices/credits/settlements. An earlier accounting cutoff never includes future facts.

Posting is a typed functional-currency journal, with original invoice/bank currencies and rate evidence on the payment. For outgoing payment: Dr AP carrying value, Cr bank actual value, difference to configured FX gain/loss. Refund reverses direction. Each project's allocation balances separately; exact bank/base distribution exhausts residual cents. The journal is immutable and cannot be changed/reversed through generic ledger APIs. No second material cost or operational consumption is created. Period FX revaluation, hedging, bank-currency remeasurement, fees, tax withholding and OCI are outside this subset.

Settlement exchange differences follow the ordinary monetary-item concept in IAS 21 ¶28–29 ([IFRS issued standard](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/pdf-standards/english/2024/issued/part-a/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates.pdf?bypass=on)); this implementation is not a claim of full IFRS or jurisdictional compliance. Historical carrying amounts are not rewritten.

A separate full reversal uses the original allocations/functional values, dated no earlier than the original and all dependent settlements. Independent approval and recording apply again. Already cleared payments must first be independently unmatched; the original cannot be reversed twice. Original outflow and correcting inflow can subsequently match their own bank rows where both occurred. Original and reversing journals remain visible. Reversal represents an evidenced correction/return, never silent deletion or a real bank instruction.

## Bank evidence

Immutable statements contain dated, signed opening/closing balances and positive receipt/outflow rows with references. Opening + inflows − outflows must equal closing. Company-wide bank access only. Reconciler must differ from each payment's recorder. One statement row can match multiple same-bank, same-direction recorded payments, exactly exhausting its bank-currency amount. Statement date cannot precede payment. Active matches are unique; stale/double matches fail atomically. Unmatching retains reason/history. Matching changes clearance only, never posts cash again. Erroneous statements may be voided by another reconciler after all active matches are removed; immutable originals remain and a new reference can replace the period. Unmatched entries remain explicit; no automatic suspense/fee posting.

## Reporting, examples and acceptance

One canonical Home/Company/Project destination. Focused preparation, review, recording and statement screens; evidence/history expand within record. Company/project findings calculated from all authorised rows through cutoff show approved plans separately from recorded outflows/refunds/reversals/cleared amounts. Currency groups never mix. AP open-item and control reconciliation include posted allocations and reversals. CSV carries scope, findings, caveats and sources. As-of clearance uses statement date; pending plans are current workflow, not historical assertions.
