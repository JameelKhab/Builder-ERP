# Client advances and recovery contract v1

29 October 2026. Original implementation plan for F092, with F077/F091/F122/F124/F133/F134/F136 and M15/M16/M23/M24/M32. This contract defines the connected target, not a claim that all stages below are implemented or that source parity is certified.

## Evidence and unresolved source behaviour

The retained `validation/receipts/source/advance.txt`, `advance-invoice.txt`, `customer-payment.txt` and `receipt-journal.txt` trace JAKH cash document 4 → invoice 31 → customer payment 13 → journal 32. The request contains contract, company, project, customer, amount, date, reason and approval. Its posted invoice credits owner advance liability rather than earned revenue. The payment settles AR separately. Certificate 4 and invoice 45 in `validation/certificates/source/` show a separate 1,000 recovery debit to advance liability. These are visible facts, not a test of source enforcement. No source application code is used.

Foreign-currency recovery, partial receipts, taxed advances, post-recovery receipt reversal, cancellation after invoicing, retention releases and final-account exceptions remain source-verification questions. Builder's rules below are explicit original controls; they must not be attributed to JAKH without further evidence.

## Request and invoice

- One request belongs permanently to an awarded contract, legal company, project, customer and contract currency. Its positive principal, dated FX, purpose, original evidence and finance-policy/account snapshots are versioned. The original advance ceiling controls aggregate approved principal, including requests already invoiced. Recovery does not replenish that ceiling. Drafts do not reserve it; concurrent approvals recheck it in one database transaction.
- Draft → submitted → approved, with return/revision and cancellation before approval. Approval is by another current authorised finance controller with a separate zero-default advance limit in company currency. A pending return is possible even when its basis is stale. The author, approving role, policy reviewer, scope, policy version, accounts and values are rechecked before commitment; stale approval requires correction, not a silent value change.
- Approval neither invoices nor records cash. An AR preparer explicitly prepares one typed advance invoice; a different AR reviewer posts under the existing separate AR limit. Request approval and AR posting are distinct decisions. Approved requests, posted invoices, evidence and history are immutable. Cancellation/credit after approval requires an explicit later correction workflow; deletion/reset is prohibited.
- Invoice source is exactly one approved certificate or one approved advance, never an artificial measurement. Existing invoice/receipt links survive schema migration. Invoice date is no earlier than the request; accounting date is no earlier than invoice and requires an open company period; dates cannot be future. Separate dated invoice FX defines ledger carrying values.
- Posting debits AR for principal plus explicitly configured exclusive tax, credits a dedicated `client_advance` liability control for principal, and credits the selected tax control for tax. It creates no work revenue, retention, procurement cost or cash. The request states its tax percentage/account and basis explicitly; no country-specific tax rule is inferred. Two-decimal currency support remains explicit. Both request and invoice review use total including tax in company currency.
- Manual journals cannot post to the advance control. Every advance invoice is a canonical client invoice, so existing partial/multi-invoice receipts, bank FX, evidence, independent recording, bank reconciliation and linked receipt corrections apply without parallel cash records.

## Settled balance and certificate recovery

- Eligible principal derives only from net recorded allocations to posted advance invoices, through the measurement cutoff. An invoice, approval, pending receipt or contract allowance is not a paid balance. For a taxed invoice, allocate cumulative paid gross to principal/tax proportionally and exhaust original residual cents on final payment. Receipt bank FX does not revalue the advance liability.
- Maintain invoice-level recovery allocations. For each advance: paid principal minus already certified recovery minus competing reservations is available. A certificate's policy recovery is capped by that available amount and current gross less retention; it cannot create a negative net. Preserve the contract recovery percentage and clearly explain the cap and unrecovered balance. Recover from eligible advances in deterministic invoice-date/ID order, retaining exact principal, tax and original functional carrying values.
- Drafts show a calculation; review stages revalidate the exact source balance. Finance certification reserves its recovery once. Separate invoice posting clears those frozen liability allocations once; certification alone creates no ledger entry. Pending certificate cancellation/return releases its provisional reservation. Approved allocations require a linked correction, never deletion.
- Recovery clears the original remaining liability carrying value, preserving final cents across partial recoveries. Do not simply multiply an old advance by a new invoice FX rate. The certificate invoice's AR/revenue/tax and recovery carrying values must balance with an explicitly evidenced FX adjustment where required. Tax-on-advance release is separate from tax on measured work; apply only the explicitly configured supported policy, never silently double tax or erase tax.
- Receipt reversal cannot reduce paid principal below protected certified recovery, including an approved but unposted certificate. Revalidate at preparation, approval and recording so a certificate approved during a pending reversal cannot be bypassed. A correction needing certificate/AR credits remains an explicit dependent workflow.

## Readable operation and acceptance

One Client advances destination leads request → invoice → receipts → recoveries, always showing company/project/contract, current fact, next owner/action and blockers. Display approved, invoiced, actually received, reserved/certified, posted recovered and outstanding separately. Evidence, tax/FX and history expand on demand. Link existing canonical invoices, receipts, certificates and journals instead of copying their screens.

Project/company reports begin with deterministic findings for the entire authorised filtered population, grouped by transaction currency with company carrying values. Reconcile posted advance principal/tax, receipt-derived eligibility and recovery allocations against their selected typed ledger sources. Preserve permission scope in report/export/search/evidence and distinguish current workflow state from cutoff monetary facts. No AI approval/posting/payment or external transmission.

Acceptance requires meaningful examples in every supplied project/company, service and authenticated HTTP journeys, exact partial/final cents, tax and foreign FX, competing approvals/recoveries, stale/revoked authority, independent decisions, closed periods, receipt reversal protection, scope/export privacy, source-to-ledger reconciliation, desktop/mobile browser checks and non-destructive saved-database recovery. Complete UI/recovery/credit/retention/parity work remains open until evidenced individually.

## Migration method

Rebuilding the two constrained tables preserves all existing columns/rows, indexes, immutability triggers and foreign-key targets inside a checked transaction. Use create-new → copy → replace → restore dependent objects → foreign-key check, with enforcement restored afterwards, following [SQLite's documented table-change procedure](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_altertable.html#otheralter). Never edit `sqlite_schema`, remove original history, or reset the workspace. Verify on a backup before upgrading the saved database.
