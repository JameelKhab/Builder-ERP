## Connected recovery now implemented

- Measurement derives available principal/tax from actual receipts through cutoff, less previously certified recovery and approved pending receipt reversals. Current gross × agreed recovery percent is capped by remaining eligible principal, net work and tax-inclusive invoice capacity. No historical catch-up is inferred.
- Every review revalidates the source quote. Independent finance certification atomically freezes allocations alongside approved work, before accounting. Uninvoiced certified deductions already reserve amounts. Returns and failed decisions leave no partial reservation.
- Explicit original-tax policy separates work tax from released advance tax. Foreign recovery requires historical-consideration policy and separate tax FX accounts. Recovered revenue uses original advance principal carrying values; original tax is cleared separately, and bank FX stays on the original receipt.
- Receipt reversals recheck certified principal/tax and competing approved correction reservations at preparation, submission, approval and recording. An overtaken draft cannot reverse protected cash. Frozen rows, source documents and posted journals remain immutable.
