## What is implemented

Schema 17 adds `client_advance_recoveries` and future policy fields (`recovery_tax_mode`, `recovery_fx_basis`, gain/loss accounts), but no allocation service or policy form consumes them. Preserve the old snapshot shape when new settings remain unconfigured so existing review cycles do not become stale without a relevant change.
2. **Exact invoice-level allocation.** Current measured gross × original contract recovery percentage, rounded once, capped by paid/unreserved principal and gross less retention. Do not infer historical catch-up. Use paid receipts/corrections through period end, less approved pending reversal reservations; a draft reversal does not reserve. Allocate deterministically by source invoice date/ID. Subtract all already certified allocations, including those with invoices not yet posted. Each source's tax and original base carrying increment equals its cumulative original ratio less prior frozen amounts, with final cents exhausted. Cap principal plus released tax against the current invoice capacity so no negative invoice is created. Use exact decimal/integer cents, with binary search if needed for the tax-inclusive cap.
3. **Certificate integration.** A draft holds a source quote/snapshot, not a permanent reservation. Revalidate source facts at every review; changes require return/revision. At independent finance approval, insert immutable recovery rows while the certificate is in finance state, in the same transaction as approved certificate lines and the state transition. Approved certificates reserve recovery immediately. Returns/cancellation before approval retain history and no permanent allocation. Existing one-pending-certificate rule remains. Remove the guard only after these controls exist.
4. **Invoice posting.** Transaction journal: debit AR, retention, each recovered advance principal and original recovered advance tax; credit measured revenue and work tax before original-tax release. Under original-tax policy, work tax uses gross or gross less retention, before advance deduction; net invoice tax is work tax minus released original advance tax. Without that policy, preserve existing semantics for untaxed advances. Convert ordinary lines at the invoice FX. Replace principal recovery base debits with frozen original carrying values and adjust measured revenue base credit by the same delta: the historical non-monetary principal difference is not FX gain/loss. Replace original-tax base debits separately; residual tax conversion differences use explicit gain/loss accounts. Keep zero-transaction base-only FX lines explicit and do not mislabel those differences as rounding. Revalidate frozen allocations and paid protection before preparation, submission and posting. Never recalculate already certified allocation amounts from a newer balance.
5. **Receipt correction protection.** At reversal preparation, submission, approval and recording, projected net paid gross less other approved reversal reservations must still cover all certified principal and tax. Exclude the current reversal from competing reservations. A draft correction may become invalid when recovery is certified; approved pending corrections reduce recovery availability. Do not delete/reset historical facts to bypass this. Credit/reversal of recovered certificates requires its own future workflow.
6. **Report and complete the user flow.** Add scoped advance list/options/findings/CSV and original carrying-value/control reconciliation. Distinguish approved, invoiced, paid, certified recovery, posted recovery and remaining amounts.
